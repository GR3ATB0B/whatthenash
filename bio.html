Who I Am

An 19-year-old Engineering student at Auburn University — currently in my first semester. Atlanta Classical Academy grad, class of 2026. I design wireless systems with Arduino and ESP32s, 3D-print custom enclosures, experiment with Raspberry Pi, and I'm always learning CAD to bring ideas to life.

At the core of everything is my faith — my projects, my leadership, and my drive all flow from that foundation. I believe in using my gifts to serve something greater than myself.

What I Do

I'm a supervisor at Chick-fil-A, where I use my Spanish daily with the kitchen staff. On the field, I play center on the football team — leading by example in the weight room and on game day. I'm also in choir and compete in engineering competitions.
